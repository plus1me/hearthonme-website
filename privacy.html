2. Data we collect

Account

  • Phone number, verified by SMS code, and your password. The password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form by our authentication provider; we cannot read it.

Profile

  • Required: name, date of birth (to check you are 18+ and to show your age), gender and the gender you are looking for, and at least one photo.
  • Optional: more photos, about me, occupation, height, education, languages, interests, zodiac sign, relationship goal, children, smoking and drinking habits, religion and political views.

Location

  • If you allow location access, your device's GPS position. We use it to work out the distance to other users, for the distance filter, to show your city (for example "Berlin, Germany") and to check attendance at a planned date. You can turn location access off in your browser or phone settings.

Use of the app

  • Your likes and passes, the ratings you give (appearance, interest, lifestyle), matches and unmatches.
  • Chat messages you send and receive.
  • Dates: the place, day and time you propose, hearts (date credits) given and used, HoM codes, cancellations with their reason, and attendance checks.
  • Hearts balance, VIP status and rewards.
  • Safety reports you submit (topic, description, optional screenshot), blocks, and the phone number of the account that sent the report.
  • Your settings: discovery filters, notification settings and app language.

Technical data

  • A device token for push notifications (iOS app), your IP address and basic device and browser information, which our providers process when you connect, verify your phone number or load maps.

3. What other users see

HeartOnMe is a dating service, so your profile is shown to other users: your name, age, photos, city, approximate distance, the profile details you chose to fill in, and your ratings summary. Your matches also see your messages and the dates you plan together. Other users are not shown your phone number, your date of birth or your exact GPS position.

Please only add information you are comfortable sharing with other members.

5. Sensitive information

Religion and political views are optional profile fields. If you fill them in, they are visible to other users. The gender you are looking for may also allow conclusions about your sexual orientation. These are special categories of data under Art. 9 GDPR. We process them only because you choose to enter them, and only to show them on your profile and use them in other users' filters. You can remove them at any time in Edit profile; this does not affect processing before you removed them.

6. Service providers

We use the following providers, who process data on our behalf under data processing agreements or, where stated, as independent controllers:

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC): sign-in and SMS verification, database, photo storage, server functions and push notifications. Data is stored on Google servers, including in the United States.
  • Google reCAPTCHA and Google Play Integrity: protect phone verification against abuse. They process technical data about your device and browser.
  • Google Maps Platform: the map and nearby places when you plan a date, and turning your position into a city name. Your position or search area and your IP address are sent to Google.
  • OpenAI (OpenAI, L.L.C., USA): only when you tap the ✨ AI button in a chat, the last five messages of that conversation (yours and your match's), your match's first name and the app language are sent to OpenAI to generate reply suggestions. Nothing is sent if you do not use the button.
  • Apple (iOS app only): delivers push notifications.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store: distribute the apps. They process data under their own privacy policies as independent controllers.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA): hosts this website and the web app. When you visit, GitHub processes your IP address and technical request data to deliver the pages and keep them secure.
  • Google Fonts (Google): the website loads its font from Google servers, which receive your IP address.

We share data with authorities only when we are legally required to, for example on a court order, or when it is necessary to protect someone's life or safety.

7. Transfers outside the EU

Some providers above process data in the United States. Transfers take place on the basis of the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, and otherwise on the basis of the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

8. How long we keep data

  • While you have an account: we keep your account and profile data until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account (My profile → Delete account): your profile, photos, ratings, likes and dates are deleted. Messages you sent may remain in the chat history of the people you wrote to, as part of their conversation.
  • Kept after deletion: your phone number, the date of deletion and your VIP status are kept for 12 months to prevent abuse, such as blocked users registering again, and to handle legal claims. Then they are deleted.
  • After an unmatch: the match record is kept for 12 months so that reports about that contact can still be investigated.
  • Safety reports: kept for 12 months after the case is closed, longer only if needed for legal proceedings.

Delete your account

In the app (Android, iOS or web app): open My profile → Delete account, confirm with the SMS code sent to your phone number, and your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app: if you can no longer use the app, send a deletion request by post to Plus1me UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Kollwitzstraße 76, 10435 Berlin, Germany, stating the phone number of your account. You can also ask for only some of your data to be deleted without deleting the account: choose Safety center → Privacy request in the app.

What is deleted: your profile, photos, ratings, likes, matches and dates. What is kept: messages you sent may remain in the conversation history of the people you wrote to, and your phone number, deletion date and VIP status are kept for 12 months to prevent abuse and handle legal claims, then deleted (see section 8).

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy (Art. 15),
  • correct it (Art. 16); you can change most of it yourself in Edit profile,
  • have it deleted (Art. 17); you can delete your account yourself in the app,
  • restrict processing (Art. 18),
  • receive your data in a portable format (data portability, Art. 20),
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21),
  • withdraw your consent at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

To use these rights, send a request through Safety center → Privacy request in the app, or by post (see section 1). We answer within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the authority responsible for us: Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin.

10. Website, cookies and storage

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies and no analytics tools. The web app saves your chosen language in your browser's local storage, and our sign-in provider stores a login token so that you stay logged in. Both are necessary for the service you request (§ 25(2) TDDDG) and stay on your device.

11. Age limit

HeartOnMe is only for people aged 18 and over. We check the date of birth at sign-up and delete accounts of users we find to be under 18. See our Age Policy.

12. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access to data is restricted by sign-in and database rules, passwords are stored hashed, and phone numbers are verified by SMS. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and report anything suspicious through the Safety center.

13. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change affects you significantly, we will let you know in the app.
