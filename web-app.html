How a date happens

Every match on HeartOnMe is a step towards meeting in person. This is the whole path, from first look to closed date.

  1. Rate before you like

    Score each profile from 1 to 10 for appearance, interest and lifestyle. A like only counts after you've rated.

  2. Match

    When you both like each other, it's a match. You can message and see each other's ratings.

  3. Agree on place and time

    You each propose a café, bar or restaurant rated above 3, plus a day and a time. When both offers are the same, the date is confirmed.

  4. Meet and swap codes

    Each of you gets a 9-digit HoM code. Enter your date's code to close the date and receive their hearts.

Made for meeting, not endless chatting

Hearts show you mean it

Send hearts on an open date to show how interested you are. Each heart uses one date credit, so they carry weight.

Only well-rated public places

Dates take place at cafés, bars and restaurants rated above 3 on Google. Pick from a map of places near you.

Fair cancellation

Cancel up to 24 hours before the date and you both get your hearts back. Three cancellations pause an account for three weeks.

Safety built in

Report or block someone from their match card. If your date doesn't show up, the Not Attend check confirms you were there.

Questions about the web app

Something else on your mind? Contact us or read the help page.

What do I need to sign up?

A mobile number from an EU country or Türkiye that can receive SMS. We send you a 6-digit code to verify it, then you choose a password. You must be at least 18, see our age policy.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open heartonme.com/app in an up-to-date browser on your phone or computer. On iPhone, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen to keep it one tap away.

Is it free?

Yes, everything is free. You start with 3 hearts, get your match's hearts back when you close a date with their HoM code, and earn a month of VIP plus 10 hearts after 2 closed dates. How hearts work

Which languages are available?

English, German and Turkish. The app follows your browser's language, and you can change it in Settings.
