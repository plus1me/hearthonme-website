We hear you through the app.

Questions, feedback, a problem with a date or a report about another user: everything reaches our team through the Safety Center in the HeartOnMe app. You can follow the status of your message there too.

  1. Open the HeartOnMe app and log in. No account yet? Create one with your phone number.
  2. Go to My profile, then Safety center.
  3. Choose a topic and describe what happened. For questions and feedback, pick Other. You can attach a screenshot.
  4. Tap Submit report. Your latest case and its status appear under Case status.
Read the FAQ

Reporting a specific person? Open their match card, tap the menu and choose Block & Report. They are blocked and unmatched right away.
